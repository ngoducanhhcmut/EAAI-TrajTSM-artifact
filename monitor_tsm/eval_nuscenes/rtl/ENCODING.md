A smaller cover is not automatically sound for every candidate accepted by the RTL sweep: it would require checking every such candidate's horizon/coverage as an additional fixed-point condition. We use the permitted largest-horizon construction, rather than claim it is the uniquely minimal cover. This can conservatively change the selected candidate versus per-candidate VA, so both `per_candidate_va_cid` and `max_cover_cid` are recorded.
